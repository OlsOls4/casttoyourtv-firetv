Cast to Your TV · Fire TV

Send a video to your TV

Enter the 6-character code on your TV, paste a video link and (optionally) subtitles. Then press Select on the remote, or ask out loud from this page, to Ask the TV about what you're watching — spoiler-free.

Ask the TV, from your phone

Tap the mic and ask out loud, or type. The TV pauses and answers on screen, using only the subtitles up to now: no spoilers.
