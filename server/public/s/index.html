Cast to Your TV · Fire TV

Send a video to your TV

Enter the 6-character code on your TV, paste a video link and (optionally) subtitles. Then press Select on the remote, or ask out loud from this page, to Ask the TV about what you're watching — spoiler-free.

Who's watching?

The TV answers you by name, in your language.

Ask the TV, from your phone

Tap the mic and ask out loud, or type. The TV pauses and answers on screen, using only the subtitles up to now: no spoilers. The same answer also appears here, so you can try it without a Fire TV (no code needed).

What did I miss?Who is she talking to?What does that line mean?
Stepping out?

Tap before you leave. When you're back, the TV pauses and catches you up on exactly what you missed: nothing after.
